Access

Can’t sign in?

  1. Confirm you are using the work email address invited by your organisation.
  2. Check that your internet connection can reach DrillOps.
  3. Ask your organisation’s DrillOps administrator to confirm that your account is active.
Workspace

Clients and projects

DrillOps only displays clients and projects assigned to your account. If a project is missing, your client or organisation administrator must grant access.

DAR workflow

Submission or approval help

Save the report, verify the selected client and project, then submit it for review. Rejected reports remain available for correction and resubmission. Contact the project manager if an approval is delayed.

Notifications

Missing alerts

Check the Notifications screen and your notification preferences. Email delivery also depends on your organisation’s configured mail service and may appear in junk or quarantine folders.

Contact

Get assistance

DrillOps accounts are issued and managed by participating organisations. For account access, project membership, report corrections, data requests or technical support, contact your organisation’s DrillOps administrator and include:

  • your work email and organisation;
  • the client and project name;
  • the approximate time the issue occurred; and
  • a screenshot that does not expose passwords or confidential information.

FieldMaps access

Use the account assigned by your organisation. Ask your administrator to confirm that you have access to the relevant published map. After a new version is published, refresh the app to retrieve it.
