Keep actuals and estimates clear.

A fuller view of the financial position.

Estimated accruals

Record and review estimated accruals separately from invoice actuals. Open accruals can contribute to the financial overview while retaining their identity as estimates, so an uninvoiced amount is not mistaken for a received invoice.

Reconciliation

Use the reconciliation workspace to compare financial records with the available operational evidence. Investigate differences using the underlying detail rather than treating an operational estimate and an invoice as interchangeable.

Project context

Work within the client, project and program available to your account. The shared context links Account with EOS and Project, while keeping the ledger, operational charges and planning allocations in their respective workspaces.

QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

What is the difference between an invoice and an accrual?

The ledger contains actual invoice and credit records. An accrual is a separate estimate for a cost that has not yet been invoiced; it is labelled and reviewed separately.

Where do drilling charges come from?

EOS holds the recorded operational work and its charge detail. Account provides the financial records and reconciliation views used to investigate the relationship between work recorded and invoices received.

Can everyone see every project?

No. Access follows the organisation account and its assigned client and project permissions. Ask your administrator if a project or supplier record is unavailable.
