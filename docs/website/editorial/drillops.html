THE WORKFLOW

From the shift record
to the agreed charges.

A daily report explains more than the metres drilled. Keep the hours, crew, consumables and delays available when the work is reviewed.

01 /

Record the shift.

Capture the rig, hole, depths, activity timeline, crew and consumables. Save a draft or submit the report when it is ready.

02 /

Review with context.

Approve a report, raise a query or reject it with a reason. Keep the review history with the work it describes.

03 /

Understand the charges.

Inspect the recorded quantities and time against contract rates. Follow the detail from the report into the charge breakdown.
