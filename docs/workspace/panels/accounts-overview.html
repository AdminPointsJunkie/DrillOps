What these figures mean

Actuals come from the invoice ledger, excluding accruals and opening budget adjustments. The net total is not the amount deducted from the annual budget. Credits reduce actuals but do not count as a received invoice. Blank dates and missing amounts are flagged, not treated as evidence of receipt.

Priced EOS charges comes from the monthly reconciliation summary. A missing invoice means no matching invoice is recorded for that contractor, project, program and month; billing delays or incomplete records may explain it. Invoice dates are not upload dates.
