FieldOps Privacy Policy

Updated 19 November 2026

You can use FieldOps without an account. If you choose to connect FieldOps to FieldMaps, you sign in with a separate FieldMaps account and can work with projects your organisation has made available to you.

Data collection

DrillOps uses FieldMaps account and sync data to provide the connection, enforce workspace permissions, save edits and prevent duplicate uploads. DrillOps does not sell or rent this data or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Device permissions

The app may request access to Location, Camera, Motion and Photos. Location is used for coordinates, mapping, waypoints, navigation and user-initiated track recording. Camera is used for field photography. Motion is used for compass and angle instruments. Photos Add access is used only when you ask the app to save a photo. Standalone field records are processed on the device. If you include coordinates in an edit to a connected FieldMaps layer and sync the edit, those coordinates are uploaded with the edit.

Field records and sharing

Tracks, waypoints, imported DXF layers and settings are stored locally on your device. Annotated photos are saved to your photo library when you request it. You can export, share or back up field records through Files. Apple services such as iCloud device backup may also retain a copy under your own Apple account settings.

Optional FieldMaps connection and sync

Signing in to FieldMaps lets you download assigned published projects for offline use. If your workspace administrator enables uploads for your account, selecting Sync now sends saved edits to supported vector layers to FieldMaps. Saving an edit does not upload it automatically. An upload can include original and edited feature coordinates and attributes, together with your FieldMaps account identifier and the identifiers for the project, layer and edit.

Edits are stored in the workspace draft. A workspace editor publishes the draft before those changes become available to other authorised devices. FieldMaps retains records of sync requests and results to prevent duplicate edits and resolve conflicts.

Apple Maps

The map uses Apple's MapKit service. Apple's handling of map service information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and platform terms.

Retention and deletion

You can delete tracks, waypoints and DXF layers inside the app. You can remove saved photographs in the Photos app. Deleting FieldOps removes its locally stored application data, subject to any device backups managed by you through Apple. It does not remove records already synced to FieldMaps. Contact your organisation’s DrillOps administrator or the address below about access or deletion of workspace records. See the DrillOps Privacy Policy for more about retention and deletion of hosted records.

Children

DrillOps does not knowingly collect personal information from children through FieldOps.

Changes

When this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
